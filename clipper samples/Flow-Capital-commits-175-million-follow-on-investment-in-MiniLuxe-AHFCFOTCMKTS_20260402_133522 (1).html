Flow Capital commits $1.75 million follow-on investment in MiniLuxe

Apr 01, 2026, 5:07 PM ETFlow Capital Corp. (AHFCF) Stock, MNLX:CA Stock, FW:CA Stock, MNLXF StockBy: Soumendra Mukherjee, SA News Editor
  • Comments
Follow us on Google for the latest stock newsFollow Seeking Alpha on Google for the latest stock news
  • Flow Capital (FW:CA) on Wednesday said it has made a fourth follow-on investment in MiniLuxe Holding Corp., committing up to $1.75 million, with an initial tranche of $1.35 million.
  • The company said if fully drawn, the total investment in MiniLuxe would reach $7.925 million across four funding rounds, supporting expansion of its nail studio network, product line, and potential franchising or acquisitions.
  • AHFCF closed +1.60% at $0.57.
